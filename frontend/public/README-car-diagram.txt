Drop your own car damage diagram here as:

    car-diagram.png

It prints on the vehicle hire agreement, in the box beside the vehicle
details, for marking existing damage at handover.

Any web image format works if you rename it to car-diagram.png. A wide
image of roughly 3:2 works best - the same proportions as the four-view
sheet (top, front, rear and side).

Until a file is present the contract falls back to a drawn outline, so
the agreement is never missing its diagram.
